# Hi, I'm Pepe

I build AI agents and the products around them. Based in Madrid.

<br>

<img src="assets/comillas.png" width="16" height="16" align="absmiddle"> &nbsp;MSc in Big Data & Advanced Analytics at ICAI, Universidad Pontificia Comillas.

<img src="assets/ebury.png" width="16" height="16" align="absmiddle"> &nbsp;Data at [Ebury](https://www.ebury.com), building models that help the business make better decisions.

<img src="assets/otel.svg" width="16" height="16" align="absmiddle"> &nbsp;**Building**: [otel](https://www.tuotel.app/), an MCP and agent that helps you sell your hotel rooms. It researches Booking prices stay by stay, so you can sell every room at the right price. It also helps with your reviews, tracks your competitors and more. Status: in development.

<img src="assets/feedo.png" width="16" height="16" align="absmiddle"> &nbsp;**Shipped**: [Feedo](https://apps.apple.com/es/app/feedo/id6806554868), a social map for restaurants. See where your friends ate and what they would order again. Live on the App Store.

**Hackathons**: [Zhivel](https://github.com/pdsdm/hackspain), an agent system for live event crises at HackSpain 2026 (HappyRobot track). [FactNews](https://github.com/pdsdm/FactNews), a news checker built on a council of LLMs and RAG at HackEurope 2026 in Dublin.
